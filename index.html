Mobile Notary Services

Mobile notary services are available by appointment in Union County and Middlesex County, New Jersey. Limited Essex County appointments may be available based on scheduling, distance, and availability.

A travel/booking fee is required for all mobile notary appointments and must be paid upon scheduling appointment. Essex County appointments are subject to a higher travel fee than Union County and Middlesex County appointments. Notary fees are separate and depend on the document type and number of notarizations required.

  • Union County mobile notary appointments
  • Middlesex County mobile notary appointments
  • Limited Essex County appointments with higher travel fee
  • General notarizations
  • Real estate documents
  • Power of attorney documents
  • Affidavits and consent forms
  • Travel/booking fee required before appointment confirmation
