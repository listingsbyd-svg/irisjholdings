Client Intake Questions

When you reach out, these details help me understand your needs and assist you properly.

Buyer Details

  • Are you pre-approved?
  • What is your budget?
  • What towns are you considering?
  • How many bedrooms and bathrooms do you need?
  • What is your timeline?

Seller Details

  • What is the property address?
  • Is the property occupied or vacant?
  • When would you like to sell?
  • What is your reason for selling?
  • Do you have a desired sale price?

Notary Details

  • What document needs notarization?
  • How many signers?
  • Where should the appointment take place?
  • What date and time do you prefer?
