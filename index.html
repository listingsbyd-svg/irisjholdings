Let’s Start With Your Goals

The more I understand your needs, budget, timeline, and concerns, the better I can prepare for our conversation and help you move forward.

Buyer Questions

  • Are you pre-approved?
  • What is your budget?
  • What towns are you considering?
  • What are your must-haves?
  • What is your timeline?

Seller Questions

  • What is the property address?
  • When would you like to sell?
  • Is the property occupied or vacant?
  • What is your ideal sale price?
  • What is your reason for selling?

Notary Questions

  • What document needs notarization?
  • How many signers?
  • Where should the appointment take place?
  • What date and time do you prefer?
